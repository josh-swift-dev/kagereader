Kage Reader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Kage Reader is a comic and manga reader designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Kage Reader does not require an account or ask you to provide personal information to read your locally stored comics.

Your imported comics, bookmarks, reading progress, collections, covers, preferences, and reading statistics are stored locally on your device.

2. Comic Files

Kage Reader accesses only the files you choose to import. Imported files are used to display and organize your personal comic library.

Kage Reader does not operate a remote service that stores your comic library.

3. Wi-Fi Transfer

Kage Reader includes an optional Wi-Fi transfer feature that allows you to upload comics from a computer to your iPhone.

When enabled, the application temporarily operates a local HTTP server.

Wi-Fi transfers are not encrypted and do not require a password. Anyone who can reach the transfer address on your local network may be able to upload files while the server is running.

Use this feature only on trusted private networks and stop the server when finished.

4. In-App Purchases

Kage Reader offers an optional one-time Pro purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple.

Kage Reader checks purchase entitlements to enable purchased features. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

5. Device Permissions

Kage Reader may request access to your local network to enable Wi-Fi transfers.

If you enable Face ID protection, authentication is handled by Apple's operating system. Kage Reader does not receive or store your biometric information.

6. Advertising

Kage Reader does not display third-party advertisements.

7. Your Control

You decide which comics to import and when to enable Wi-Fi transfer.

You can delete imported books from the application, change permissions in iOS Settings, or uninstall the application.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change.
