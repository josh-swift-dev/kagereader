Kage Reader Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Your library is your business.

Kage Reader is designed as a private comic and manga reader for files you choose to import.

Information We Collect

Kage Reader does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information in order to use the app.

Your Comic and Manga Files

Kage Reader accesses files only when you choose to import them into the app. Imported comics, images, covers, reading progress, favorites, and other library information are stored locally on your device.

Kage Reader does not upload your personal comic library to a Kage Reader server.

Supported Files

Kage Reader may access supported files that you explicitly select, including PDF, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, JPG/JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and WebP files.

In-App Purchase

Kage Reader allows users to add up to three books for free. An optional one-time Unlimited Unlock removes this library limit.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Kage Reader does not receive or store your credit card or other payment information.

Analytics and Advertising

Kage Reader does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Kage Reader does not sell or rent your personal information. Files are shared outside the app only when you choose to use a sharing feature provided by your device.

Children's Privacy

Kage Reader does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Kage Reader changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.
