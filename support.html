Supported File Types

PDF CBZ CBR ZIP JPG JPEG PNG HEIC WebP

How to Import a Comic

  1. Open Kage Reader.
  2. Tap the + button in the upper-right corner.
  3. Select a supported file from the Files picker.
  4. The comic will be added to your library.
  5. Tap its cover to view the book and begin reading.

Free Library Limit

Kage Reader allows you to add up to three books for free so you can try the complete reading experience before purchasing the Unlimited Unlock.

Unlimited Unlock

Unlimited Unlock is a one-time $1.99 in-app purchase that removes the three-book library limit.

This is a permanent, non-consumable unlock and is not a subscription. There are no monthly or annual fees.

Restore Purchase

If you previously purchased Unlimited Unlock and reinstall Kage Reader or move to another compatible device, use the Restore Purchases option in Kage Reader while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Reading Progress

Kage Reader automatically remembers your reading position so you can return to a comic and continue where you left off.

CBZ, CBR and ZIP Files

Comic archives should contain supported image files. Some damaged, encrypted, unusually compressed, or unsupported archives may not be readable.

Having Trouble Importing?

If a comic does not import:

  • Confirm that the file uses a supported format.
  • Make sure the file is fully downloaded to your device.
  • Verify that the archive is not password protected or damaged.
  • Try opening another comic file to determine whether the issue is specific to that file.

Deleting a Comic

You can remove an imported comic from your Kage Reader library. Deleting a book from Kage Reader does not cancel or remove your Unlimited Unlock purchase.
