Kage Reader - Support

Welcome ALL to Kage Reader Support. This guide explains how to import, organize, and read your comics.

1. Getting Started

  1. Launch Kage Reader.
  2. Use the import button to add your comics.
  3. Tap a comic cover to begin reading.
  4. If you have previously opened the book, choose whether to continue reading or start over.

2. Supported File Formats

Kage Reader is designed to support:

  • PDF
  • CBZ
  • CBR
  • CB7
  • ZIP
  • Image-based comic EPUB files
  • JPG and JPEG
  • PNG
  • HEIC
  • WebP

Compatibility may vary depending on the file's structure and compression.

3. Wi-Fi Transfer

You can transfer comics directly from your computer to your iPhone.

  1. Connect your computer and iPhone to the same trusted private Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Kage Reader and navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Wi-Fi Transfer.
  4. Start the transfer server.
  5. Enter the displayed address into your computer's browser.
  6. Select the files you want to upload.
  7. Monitor upload progress in your computer's browser.
  8. Stop the server when finished.

Wi-Fi Transfer does not require a password and uses unencrypted local HTTP. Only use it on trusted private networks.

4. Reading Your Comics

Kage Reader offers several reading options, including traditional comic reading, right-to-left manga reading, continuous scrolling, and supported two-page layouts.

You can adjust your reading experience through Reader Settings.

For an explanation of each reader button, open the Reader Button Guide in the application's Settings menu.

5. Changing a Comic's Cover

  1. Open the comic.
  2. Navigate to your preferred page.
  3. Tap the Set Current Page as Cover symbol in the reader controls.
  4. Confirm your selection when prompted.

6. Managing Collections

You can organize your comics into collections and series.

Edit a comic's information to assign it to a collection.

Open Settings and select Manage Collections to rename or remove existing collections.

7. Free Version and Pro

The intended free version allows up to three imported books at a time.

Deleting an existing book frees a slot for another import.

Kage Reader Pro offers unlimited imports through a one-time purchase.

8. Restoring Purchases

  1. Confirm that you are signed in to the Apple Account used for your original purchase.
  2. Open the purchase area in Kage Reader.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.

If your purchase still cannot be restored, contact support.

9. Troubleshooting

My comic will not import

Check that the file is fully downloaded, uses a supported format, and is not corrupted.

Wi-Fi Transfer will not connect

Confirm that both devices are connected to the same network.

Check that Kage Reader has local-network permission and that the transfer server is running.

My upload stops unexpectedly

Keep Kage Reader open during the transfer.

Make sure your computer does not enter sleep mode and that your network connection is stable.

My comic displays incorrectly

Try changing the reading direction or display mode in Reader Settings.
